# Complaints and extra support

If you are unhappy with our service you can make a complaint by phone, email or letter. We acknowledge complaints within 5 working days and send a final response within 8 weeks.

If you are not happy with our final response, you can refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service, which is free to use.

If you are going through a difficult time, such as a bereavement or serious illness, our Customer Support Team can give extra help. Ask to be transferred to the Customer Support Team when you call.
