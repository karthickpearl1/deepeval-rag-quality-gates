# Payments and cancellation

You can pay for your policy annually by card or monthly by direct debit. Paying monthly includes a credit charge, which is shown on your quote.

You have a 14 day cooling-off period from the start of your policy. If you cancel within the cooling-off period and have not made a claim, you get a full refund.

If you cancel after the cooling-off period, we refund the unused part of your premium minus a £30 cancellation fee.
