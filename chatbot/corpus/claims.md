# Making a claim

You can make a home or motor claim online through your account or by calling the claims line on 0808 157 0123. The claims line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

When you make a claim we will ask for your policy number, the date of the incident and photos of any damage. For theft or vandalism you will also need a crime reference number from the police.

We aim to make a claim decision within 5 working days of receiving all the documents we need. Once a claim is agreed, payment is made by bank transfer within 3 working days.
